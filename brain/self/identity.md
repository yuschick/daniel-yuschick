## Background

_Sources: talk transcripts; [Smashing Magazine Person of the Week](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/person-of-the-week/daniel-yuschick/) (9 September 2024)._

- Originally from the US; lives in Helsinki, Finland.
- Building websites for over 25 years, starting in Notepad. First job title: assistant web master ("or assistant to the web master, depending on who you asked").
- Grew up writing HTML and CSS, and spent years between design and development ("not designer enough for the design team, not techy enough to be a developer"), which led to UI, UX, accessibility and then design systems.
- Taught front-end engineering to career changers in the US.

## Work

- **Current title:** Lead Design System Developer (speaker bio).
- **Posti**, Finland's national postal service: lead design systems developer.
- **Finland's largest banking institution:** design system lead.
- **SAP:** a short stint ("my short time at SAP", zeroheight article).
- Led the renewal of a legacy design system, from a monolithic v89 to a new v1 with predictable release cycles (zeroheight article; the organization isn't named there).
- Previously at a gaming and streaming company, building highly interactive UI.
- Both Posti and the bank fall under the European Accessibility Act.
