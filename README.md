Daniel Yuschick is a design systems lead, architect, and engineer who turns design intent into accessible, AI-ready systems that teams actually adopt. With over 20 years of software experience, he has most recently led design system work at OP Pohjola, Posti, and Supermetrics, with a focus on European Accessibility Act (EAA) compliance and AI-driven workflows. He writes about design systems and the frontend for Smashing Magazine, speaks internationally at events including Zeroheight's Converge and Deque's axe-con, was Smashing Magazine’s Person of the Week in September 2024, is an active member of the Redwoods design system community, and mentors through the Helsinki non-profits Hive Helsinki and Codebar. Outside of work, he writes horror fiction and has a love for great chocolate and beautiful tattoos.
